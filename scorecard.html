Scorecard · Solar Fuel Ltd

Graded On Fourteen Things. You Pass The Hard Ones And Fail The Easy Ones.

Prepared for Matthew Hantschar · 5 October 2026 · AG1 Consulting
C−

Measured on 5 October 2026 from solar-fuel.co.uk. Six grades at A or B, six at E or F, and nothing in between. ⭐ That spread is the whole story: the expensive, slow work is done well and the cheap, fast work is not done at all.

⚠️ This grades the website, not the business. AG1 has not seen a single figure from Solar Fuel and makes no judgement about how the company performs.

⭐ What You Already Do Better Than Almost Anyone
 What we checkedWhat we found
A Accreditation MCS, NAPIT, TrustMark and RECC, all four on the site. ⭐ MCS gates the Smart Export Guarantee and most funding, RECC is the consumer code, TrustMark is government endorsed, NAPIT covers the electrical side. Most installers cannot show all four.
A Content depth Thirty pages, 1,722 words on the homepage. Most installers we audit have six pages and a gallery.
A Blog Present and maintained. ⭐ Almost nobody in this trade keeps one going.
A Code structure Heading tags balanced, one H1 per page. Sounds trivial. Two of the four sites we audited this week have broken heading tags.
B Team page Present. A buyer can see who they are dealing with.
B Reviews and guarantee Both on the site. ⚠️ Volume and recency not assessed.
B Service breadth Solar, battery and storage, and commercial work all named and separated.
🔴 What Is Costing You, Cheapest Fix First
 What we checkedWhat we found
F Air conditioning 🔴🔴 No mention of air conditioning anywhere on the site. Not a page, not a sentence. ⭐ If air con is work you want, you are invisible for it to customers searching and to engineers looking for a job. ⚠️ If it is not work you want, tell us and this row disappears.
F Careers page 🔴 None. No vacancies page, nothing aimed at somebody who might want to work there. ⭐ A properly accredited firm is an attractive place to work and nothing says so.
F Enquiry forms 🔴 One form across thirty pages. The content does the persuading and then has nowhere to land. Cheapest fix with the clearest effect on this page.
F Conversion tracking 🔴 No conversion event detected on form submission or phone tap. So you cannot tell how many people enquired last month, or from which page.
F Google Ads tag 🔴 None. There is a Meta pixel, so somebody prepared social advertising deliberately. Search has never been tagged from this site. ⚠️ Not proof you are not advertising — campaigns can run through an agency container. It is our first question.
F Local business markup 🔴 The only structured data on the site describes the website itself. No address, no postcode, no opening hours, no coordinates where search engines read them. An hour or two of work.
E Tap-to-call Two tap-to-call links across thirty pages. ⚠️ Most people read a trade site on a phone.
E Email link 🔴 One published address reads %20info@solar-fuel.co.uk — an encoded space has got into the link. Opens blank or fails depending on the device. Minutes to fix, and the people it fails for simply go away.
⚠️ Could Not Grade
 What we checkedWhy no grade
? Analytics Google Tag Manager is installed. ⚠️ Tag Manager is a container, not a measurement tool, and we cannot see inside it from outside. If you can answer "how many people enquired last month and from which page", it is wired up. If not, it is sitting empty.
? Accreditation currency We read four names off your website. ⛔ We have made no enquiry of MCS, NAPIT, TrustMark or RECC and have not verified that any is current.
? Page speed and mobile Not measured in this pass.
⭐ The Pattern Worth Seeing
⭐ You have done the part that takes years. You have skipped the part that takes an afternoon

Four accreditations take years and real money to hold. Thirty pages and a maintained blog take sustained effort. A team page takes a decision to be visible. You have all of it.

🔴 And then a form missing from twenty-nine pages, no tracking on anything, no local business details in the code, and a broken email link. ⭐ Every one of those is a few hours of work.

⭐⭐ Which is unusual and it is good news. Most firms we audit have the opposite problem: a slick website with nothing behind it. You have the substance and no plumbing, and plumbing is quick.

If You Fixed Three Things
  • 1. Conversion tracking on what you already have. You own Tag Manager already. Until something counts, neither of us can tell whether anything else worked, including anything you might pay us for.
  • 2. A form on every page, and tap-to-call throughout. Thirty pages of persuasion with one place to act on it is the single biggest gap on the list.
  • 3. The air conditioning question. ⭐ If it is work you want, it needs a page. It is the only finding here that serves both halves of what you told us — customers cannot find it, and neither can an engineer looking for a job.
None of those three is a marketing campaign. ⭐ They are the things that have to be true before a campaign can be judged at all.

Four accreditations, thirty pages, a maintained blog, and one enquiry form.

The hard part is done. The easy part is not.
