🔴 The Finding, And It Is One Number
🔴🔴 Forty to fifty per cent of your website enquiries book, and eight or nine out of ten of those buy

We audit a lot of trade websites. ⭐⭐ We have not seen a conversion rate like that before. Most installers would be pleased with a quarter of enquiries booking and a third of those closing.

Yours is roughly four times that, and it holds because of things you have already paid for: MCS, NAPIT, TrustMark and RECC on the site, thirty pages of real content, a maintained blog, a team page, reviews and a guarantee. By the time somebody fills in your form they have already decided you are credible.

🔴 And you are not advertising. So the best-converting part of your business is the one part nobody is feeding.

What One Website Enquiry Is Actually Worth

Your own figures, multiplied out

£8,000 × 45% × 85% ≈ £3,060

Average job £8,000. Forty-five per cent of website enquiries book a survey. Eighty-five per cent of those convert. So every enquiry that reaches your website is worth about £3,060 in revenue, and roughly £612 in net profit at your 20%.

⚠️ Midpoints of the ranges you gave us. Use your own ends and it moves between roughly £2,560 and £3,600.

⭐⭐ Now set that against Bark at £40 a lead. Even if a Bark lead converts at a fraction of a website lead, the arithmetic is not close. The question is not whether paid acquisition works for you. It is why nobody has pointed any at the thing that converts at eighty-five per cent.

⭐⭐ Six more enquiries a week is the whole gap. You already get two or three from the website with no advertising behind it at all. This is not a transformation. It is roughly tripling one channel that already works.

⚠️ And the honest caveat: that assumes advertised enquiries convert like your organic ones. They usually convert a bit worse, because somebody who searched for you has more intent than somebody shown an advert. So treat six as the floor of the number, not the target.
⚠️ The Thing That Decides Whether Any Of This Is Wise
⚠️ Capacity, and you half answered it already

You are at 2 jobs a week, on pace for 4 to 5. Four staff plus self-employed fitters, a few people waiting to join, and still looking for an air conditioning engineer.

⭐ So the capacity is arriving, which is why this conversation is worth having now rather than in six months. But it is the question to answer before any money is spent:

  • If six more enquiries a week arrived from Monday, could you install them?
  • What is the ceiling with the fitters you have plus the ones waiting to join?
  • Is the air con engineer the thing standing between you and 4 to 5 a week, or a separate problem?

⛔ If the answer is that you cannot resource it, we would say so. More enquiries into a business that cannot install them produces slower quotes and a worse reputation, and you have spent years building the reputation that gives you an eighty-five per cent conversion rate.

⭐ What We Would Not Touch
⭐ The hard part is already done and we would protect it

MCS, NAPIT, TrustMark and RECC. All four. ⭐ That combination is rare and it is a large part of why your conversion rate is what it is.

Thirty pages, a maintained blog, a team page, battery and storage covered, commercial work named, reviews and a guarantee, and a correct heading structure. Your site is the best built of anybody we are speaking to this week and we are not going to pretend otherwise to sell you a rebuild.

⭐ And 70% word of mouth is not a weakness. It is the reason the other 30% converts so well. Nothing we do should put it at risk.

What We Still Need To Know
  • 1. Are you running Google Ads anywhere? Nothing on the site says so, and it changes the plan completely.
  • 2. Can you see how many people enquired last month and from which page? Tag Manager is installed but we cannot see inside it. If you cannot answer, it is sitting empty.
  • 3. Who is the lead supplier, and what is it costing? 6 to 8 a week at 60-70% answered is a real channel and we would want to know what it converts at against the website.
  • 4. Why did Bark actually stop? ⚠️ Lead quality, or just that recommendations filled the diary? Those are very different answers.
  • 5. What is the ceiling on installs with the people you have and the ones joining?
  • 6. Is air conditioning a growing part of the business, or a specific contract?
