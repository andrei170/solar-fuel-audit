Fully Accredited, And Advertising On The Wrong Platform

Prepared for Matthew Hantschar · 5 October 2026 · AG1 Consulting
Said Upfront: We Have Not Spoken Yet
So this is a read of your website, not a diagnosis of your business

We have your name, your number and a slot at five o'clock. Nobody has told us what your problem is, what a job is worth to you, or where your work comes from.

⭐ So everything below was measured from solar-fuel.co.uk rather than assumed about you. If the thing keeping you up at night is not on this page, say so in the first two minutes and we will put it away.

⭐⭐ What You Have That Almost Nobody Does
⭐ Four accreditations, and this is genuinely the top tier

Your site carries MCS, NAPIT, TrustMark and RECC.

⭐⭐ That combination is rare and it is the whole ballgame in solar. MCS is the gate on the Smart Export Guarantee and on most grant funding. RECC is the consumer code. TrustMark is the government-endorsed scheme. NAPIT covers the electrical side. A homeowner who has done thirty minutes of reading knows to check for exactly these, and most of your competitors cannot show all four.

Alongside that:

  • Thirty pages. ⭐ That is a real site, not a brochure. Most installers we audit have six.
  • A blog, which almost nobody in this trade maintains.
  • A team page, so a buyer can see who they are dealing with.
  • Battery and storage covered, not just panels.
  • Commercial work named as well as domestic.
  • Reviews and a guarantee both present.
  • Your heading structure is correct — which sounds trivial and is not. Two of the four sites we looked at this week have broken heading tags.

⛔ We are not going to tell you your website is bad, because it is not. It is the best-built site of anybody we are speaking to this week. The findings below are about what it is not doing, not about what is wrong with it.

🔴 The Finding That Matters Most
🔴🔴 You have a Meta pixel and no Google Ads tag

We checked for every standard advertising and measurement tag. You have Google Tag Manager and a Meta pixel. ⛔ There is no Google Ads conversion tag anywhere on the site.

A Meta pixel is not installed by accident. Somebody put it there to run or to prepare Facebook and Instagram advertising. A Google Ads tag is the equivalent for search, and its absence says search advertising has not been run from this site.

⚠️ Which matters more in solar than in almost any other trade. Here is the difference:

  • On Meta you interrupt somebody who was looking at something else and try to make them want solar. It works, but you are creating the demand.
  • On Google somebody has typed “solar panel installers near me” or “MCS certified installer” or “solar battery cost”. ⭐ They have already decided. They are looking for you specifically.

⭐⭐ And your four accreditations are worth far more on Google than on Facebook. Somebody scrolling Instagram does not know what MCS is. Somebody who has searched “is MCS certification required for solar” absolutely does, and you can show them all four. Your strongest asset is invisible on the only channel you appear to be measuring.

⚠️ If you are already running Google Ads through an agency with its own tag, this finding is wrong and we would want to know in the first five minutes.

The Rest, In Order
🔴 1. One enquiry form across thirty pages

We found a single contact form on the whole site, and only two tap-to-call links.

⚠️ Thirty pages of good content and one place to act on it. Somebody reading your battery page, or a blog post about export rates, has to navigate away to find a way of contacting you — and a proportion of them will not bother.

⭐ This is the cheapest fix with the clearest effect on the list. The content is already doing the persuading; it just has nowhere to land.

🔴 2. No local business information in your site's code

The only structured data on the site describes the website itself. There is no LocalBusiness markup — no address, no postcode, no opening hours, no coordinates in the place search engines read them.

⚠️ For a trade sold on “near me” searches that is a real gap, and it is an hour or two of work with no ongoing cost.

⚠️ 3. A broken email link

One of your published addresses reads %20info@solar-fuel.co.uk — an encoded space has got into the link. Depending on the device, clicking it opens a blank message or fails.

⭐ Minutes to fix, and the sort of thing nobody notices because the people it fails for simply go away.

⚠️ 4. Tag Manager is installed. We could not see what it is firing

Tag Manager is a container rather than a measurement tool. ⚠️ We cannot tell from outside whether analytics and conversion tracking are configured inside it, or whether it is sitting there empty.

⭐ So this is a question, not a finding: can you currently answer how many people enquired last month and which page they came from? If yes, good. If not, the container is not wired up.

What We Could Not Verify
Plainly, because we have had no call
  • Anything about your business. Job value, volume, capacity, where work comes from, what you want more of. ⭐ All of it is the call.
  • Whether your accreditations are current. We read four logos and names off your website. We have made no enquiry of MCS, NAPIT, TrustMark or RECC.
  • Whether you are already advertising. The Meta pixel says somebody has prepared for it. It does not tell us whether anything is running, what it costs, or how it is going.
  • What is inside Tag Manager. See above.
  • Whether domestic or commercial is the money, and which you would rather have.
What We Would Ask At Five
  • 1. What is a typical installation worth? Domestic and commercial, if they differ.
  • 2. Where does the work come from now? Referrals, search, Meta, grant schemes, a lead broker?
  • 3. Are you running Google Ads anywhere? ⭐ Nothing on the site says so, and it changes the plan completely.
  • 4. Can you see how many people enquired last month, and from which page? If not, Tag Manager is empty.
  • 5. Which do you actually want more of — domestic, commercial, or battery retrofits?
  • 6. Could you take more work tomorrow? ⚠️ If installers are the constraint rather than enquiries, this is a different conversation and we would rather know now.
  • 7. Have you advertised before and had it go badly? The honest answer to this shapes everything.
Question three is the one that decides the call. ⭐ If search advertising has genuinely never been run from a site carrying MCS, NAPIT, TrustMark and RECC, that is the most obvious gap we have found all week.

MCS, NAPIT, TrustMark and RECC. Thirty pages. A Meta pixel and no Google Ads tag.

Your best argument is invisible on the channel where people are already looking for it.
