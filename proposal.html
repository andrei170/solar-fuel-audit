One And A Quarter Extra Jobs A Month. You Do Sixteen To Twenty.

Solar Fuel Ltd · prepared for Jon · AG1 Consulting
What we would do, and what it costs

Jon, this is what I said I would bring you. A plan built round what you already have rather than a package, a price that leaves out the things you are already paying somebody else for, and the arithmetic so you can judge it yourself.

🔴🔴 The whole argument in one line

You convert better than almost anyone we audit, and you do not advertise at all.

  • Your leads convert to a booking at 60 to 70 per cent
  • The leads you paid for, through Bark, converted at 80 per cent
  • And you turned Bark off, because word of mouth got busy

⭐⭐ So you have proof that buying leads works for you, and you stopped buying them. That is not a demand problem. It is simply that nobody is feeding the machine.

🔴🔴 The number that decides this, and it is smaller than you think

Your own figures: about £8,000 a job at 40 per cent gross. So one extra job is roughly £3,200 of gross margin.

⭐⭐ You need roughly one and a quarter extra jobs a month to stand still on this. You are already doing sixteen to twenty.

⚠ That is a break-even calculation, not a forecast, and it is not a promise. It uses your gross margin, not your net, and it assumes nothing about how many jobs advertising actually brings. I am showing you the hurdle, not clearing it for you.

🔴 The gap you told me you want to close

You said £800,000 last year, on pace for £1.1 million this year, and you want £2 million from January.

That gap is £900,000. At £8,000 a job it is about 113 more jobs in the year, which is roughly two more a week on top of the four or five you are doing.

⚠ It is close to doubling, and it will not come from word of mouth, because recommendations grow with the work you have already done rather than with the work you want. ⭐ Seventy per cent of your business arrives through a channel you cannot turn up. That is the real reason to advertise, and it has nothing to do with your website being bad.

⭐⭐ First, the part I am NOT charging you for

You already pay a company to look after the website and the SEO, and from what I measured they are doing the slow part well: your content, your blog, your accreditations, your code structure and your service breadth all graded A or B.

🔴 So I am not pitching you a website, and I am not going to tell you to sack them. Instead, here is the list to send them. It is theirs to do and it should cost you nothing:

  • An enquiry form on all 30 pages. You have one, on one page
  • Tap to call on all 30 pages. You have two
  • Fix the broken email link. One published address is encoded wrongly and opens blank or fails depending on the device. That one has been costing you enquiries silently
  • Local business markup: address, postcode and opening hours in a form search engines read. An hour or two of work
  • An air conditioning page. 🔴 Not one mention of AC exists on the site, so you are invisible to anyone searching for it, and it makes hiring an AC engineer harder too
  • Conversion tracking on the form and the phone, so next month you can answer which page produced which enquiry

⚠ If they do all six, your lead flow improves without me. I would rather tell you that than sell you a rebuild you do not need.

🔴 What I would actually do, in order
  • 1. Measurement first. Conversion tracking and a Google Ads tag, so that every pound after this is attributable. ⚠ You have a Meta pixel sitting on the site that nobody has ever used, and search has never been tagged at all
  • 2. Google first, not Meta. Somebody searching solar panel installation has already decided; somebody scrolling has not. Your conversion rate is your advantage and search plays to it
  • 3. Turn Bark back on while we build. ⭐ It converted at 80 per cent and it is already paid for per lead. There is no reason for it to be off while you wait for me
  • 4. Then Meta, once search is proven, for the bigger-ticket and commercial work
  • 5. The air conditioning engineer, through the recruitment side, as a separate piece if you want it. You said within the month, and that Matt is fitting them himself
