The Questions, And Why Each One Earns Its Place

Call with John · 5 October 2026 · AG1 Consulting
⭐ This Is The Better Call, Not The Consolation Prize
An owner guesses at his numbers. The person running the admin knows them

Matthew cannot make it and John can. That is not a downgrade for a first conversation, it is an upgrade, as long as it is treated as what it is.

  • Owners estimate. Ask a solar company owner how many enquiries came in last month and you will get a feeling. Ask the person who handles them and you get a number.
  • Admin sees the whole funnel. Enquiry, survey, quote, chase, install, invoice. The owner mostly sees the jobs that happened.
  • ⭐ Admin knows where it breaks, because they are the ones it breaks on.

⛔ So do not pitch. John cannot buy, and trying to sell to somebody who cannot buy makes them defensive and costs you the numbers. Spend the whole half hour finding out how the business actually works, and leave with the figures that let you build Matthew a real proposal.

⭐ And say that out loud at the start. “I am not going to pitch you anything today, Matthew is not here and that would be pointless. I would rather understand how it all works so that when I do speak to him I am not wasting his time.” It relaxes the whole call and it is true.

The Numbers To Leave With

These six are the ones that decide whether there is a proposal here at all. ⭐ Get these and the call was worth it, whatever else happens.

 AskWhat it tells youAnswer
1Roughly how many enquiries come in a month?The top of the funnel, and whether volume is even the problem—
2How many of those turn into a site survey?Qualification. A big drop here means the enquiries are wrong, not few—
3How many surveys become installs?The close rate. The single most useful number on this page—
4What is an average install worth?Whether the maths works at all—
5How many installs could you do a month if the work was there?Capacity. ⚠️ If they are full, this is the wrong pitch entirely—
6Where do enquiries come from, best first?Which channel actually earns, as against which one gets talked about—
Question 5 is the one people forget to ask. ⭐ If Solar Fuel is already at capacity, more enquiries make their month worse and the honest answer is to say so. Better to find that out from John now than from Matthew after a proposal.
🔴 The Three Questions Built On What We Measured
🔴 1. “Are you running Google Ads?”

We read the site. There is a Meta pixel and no Google Ads conversion tag anywhere.

Somebody installed Facebook tracking deliberately, so somebody has run or prepared social advertising. Search has never been tagged from that site.

⭐⭐ Why it matters more in solar than almost any trade: on Meta you interrupt somebody and create the want. On Google they have already typed “MCS certified installer” or “solar battery cost” and decided. And their four accreditations mean nothing to a scroller and everything to a searcher.

⚠️ John will know whether an agency is running anything, because he will have seen the invoices. Ask him that rather than asking whether they advertise.

🔴 2. “Can you tell how many people enquired last month, and from which page?”

Google Tag Manager is installed. ⚠️ Tag Manager is a container, not a measurement tool, and we cannot see from outside whether anything is configured inside it.

⭐ So ask the question that reveals it. If John can answer, it is wired up and working. If the answer is “I would have to ask” or “we just know when the phone rings”, the container is sitting there empty and they have been flying blind.

🔴 3. “What happens when an enquiry comes in?”

There is one enquiry form across thirty pages, and two tap-to-call links. So most people ring.

Walk the whole path with him: who picks it up, how fast, what gets written down, who books the survey, what happens if the customer goes quiet.

⭐⭐ This is where an admin tells you things an owner never would. If there is a gap, John is the person tripping over it every day, and he will usually volunteer it if you ask about the process rather than about the problem.

What Is Genuinely Good, And Worth Saying
⭐ Open with this. It is true and it buys you the rest of the call

MCS, NAPIT, TrustMark and RECC. All four. MCS gates the Smart Export Guarantee and most funding, RECC is the consumer code, TrustMark is government endorsed, NAPIT covers the electrical side. ⭐ Most installers cannot show all four.

Plus thirty pages, a maintained blog, a team page, battery and storage covered, commercial work named, reviews, a guarantee, and a correct heading structure.

⛔ Their site is the best built of anybody we are speaking to this week and we should say so. There is no “your website is broken” angle here, and pretending there is would be obvious to somebody who works there.

The Political Questions, Asked Gently
  • 1. What is your role day to day? ⭐ Ask it first and ask it plainly. “Main admin” could mean office manager, operations, or the person who runs everything while Matthew sells.
  • 2. How does something like this normally get decided here? Not “who decides”, which sounds like you are trying to get past him. This version invites him to explain the process and usually gets you more.
  • 3. Has anyone pitched you marketing before, and how did it go? ⚠️ If an agency has burned them, you need to know before you propose anything.
  • 4. What would Matthew say the biggest problem is? ⭐ Gets you the owner's view through somebody who is not performing for you.
  • 5. And what would you say it is? Ask both. They are often different, and the difference is the most useful thing you will hear all call.
  • 6. What does a good month look like versus a bad one? Seasonality, and whether they know their own pattern.
⭐ Questions four and five together are the whole call. If John says Matthew wants more leads and John says the problem is that nobody follows up the quotes, you have just found the real brief and the reason the last agency failed.
How To Close It
⛔ Do not ask for the sale. Ask for the next conversation

End with something like: “That is really helpful. Based on what you have told me there are one or two things I would want to show Matthew, but I would rather put something specific in front of him than a general pitch. When is he about?”

⭐ That does three things. It tells John the call mattered, it makes him an ally rather than a gatekeeper, and it gets the next meeting booked without asking him for a decision he cannot make.

⚠️ And ask whether he wants to be on that call. If he has given you the numbers, he has a stake in it going well.

Six numbers, three questions about the funnel, and who decides what.

No pitch. He cannot buy, and trying would cost you the only thing he can give you.
