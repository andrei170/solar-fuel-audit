The Short Version
⭐⭐ What you have

MCS, NAPIT, TrustMark and RECC. ⭐ That combination is rare and it is exactly what an informed buyer checks for. MCS gates the Smart Export Guarantee and most funding, RECC is the consumer code, TrustMark is government-endorsed, NAPIT covers the electrical side.

Plus thirty pages, a blog, a team page, battery and storage, commercial work named, reviews, a guarantee, and a correct heading structure. Protect all of it.

🔴 1. A Meta pixel, and no Google Ads tag anywhere

Somebody installed a Facebook pixel deliberately. There is no Google Ads conversion tag on the site.

⚠️ On Meta you interrupt somebody and create the want. On Google somebody has already typed "MCS certified installer" or "solar battery cost" and decided. ⭐⭐ Your four accreditations mean almost nothing to a scroller and everything to a searcher - so your strongest argument is sitting on the channel where nobody is asking for it.

⚠️ If you are running search ads through an agency, this is wrong and we want to know early.

🔴 2. One enquiry form across thirty pages, and two tap-to-call links

The content does the persuading and then has nowhere to land. ⭐ Cheapest fix with the clearest effect.

🔴 3. No local business information in the code

No address, postcode, hours or coordinates where search engines read them. ⚠️ For a trade sold on "near me", that is a real gap and an hour or two of work.

⚠️ 4. A broken email link

%20info@solar-fuel.co.uk - an encoded space in the link. Opens blank or fails depending on the device. Minutes to fix, and the people it fails for just go away.

The Question That Decides The Call
Are you running Google Ads anywhere? ⭐ Nothing on your site suggests it. If search advertising has genuinely never been run from a site carrying MCS, NAPIT, TrustMark and RECC, that is the most obvious gap we have found all week - and it is a gap in your favour, because the hard part is already done. If you are running it and it is going badly, that is a completely different conversation and a more interesting one.
